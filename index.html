My Story

From electrical engineering to building products people love.

My journey with technology started back in 2016, when I trained my first deep learning model - AlexNet on CCTV images. I was an electrical engineering student at IIT Delhi, but I was fascinated by what AI could do. That curiosity turned into a passion that has shaped my entire career.

Product Manager - AI/ML

Made the leap into solving problems for software products at a Med-tech company.
