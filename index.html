Investment thesis

The bull case

How I work. These are opinions, not a framework.

01

Learn one level below your job.

I took a data-science role to understand how code actually ships. Now I write better specs and get fewer surprises.

02

Pricing is product.

What you charge and how you charge it tells customers who the product is for. I treat it as a feature, not a spreadsheet.

03

Ship the smallest thing that can prove you wrong.

A wrong v1 in front of users teaches more than a perfect deck in front of execs.

04

Data says what. Customers say why.

Dashboards find the leak. Conversations explain it. I need both before I'll bet the roadmap.
